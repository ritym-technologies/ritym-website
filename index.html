PulseStage — MIDI Studio

A dedicated Windows MIDI control station, diagnostic packet monitor, master 24-PPQN clock, and driver-free practice lab designed for touring musicians, guitarists, and studio engineers.

🎛️ Precision Command Station

Send Program Changes with selectable 0–127 or 1–128 indexing without wire corruption. Full Bank Select (CC0 MSB / CC32 LSB) and tactile CC editing with explicit send protection.

⏱️ Master 24-PPQN Clock

Real-time clock engine with Start, Continue, and Stop transport commands, tap tempo, and live arrival interval tracking to diagnose host timing jitter.

📊 Bidirectional Event Monitor

Microsecond packet timestamps, port directionality tags, live CC activity curves, and clock interval graphs. Exports directly to CSV, JSON, and text.

🎹 Driver-Free Practice Lab

Built-in velocity keybed and reference receiving pedal with live 7-segment displays. Practice and verify complex presets out of the box with zero third-party drivers needed.

⚡ Macro Cue Actions & Delays

Execute multi-step preset and parameter chains with deterministic millisecond pauses to give vintage synths and digital modelers time to process patch changes.

🛡️ Receipt-Driven Test Bench

Four automated checks (Note On/Off, Preset Change, Parameter CC, Clock sync) that only PASS when confirmation bytes are parsed at the receiving port.

About Our Name & Work

The Rhythm of Things

The name ritym comes from rhythm — the steady, dependable pulse that keeps systems aligned.

Whether it's the 24 pulses per quarter note locking together synthesizers on a festival stage, or a nanosecond satellite timestamp pinpointing a coordinate on a topo map, precision timing is the foundation of every honest signal.

We are an independent software and hardware studio. We keep our team small, our overhead modest, and our claims true. We build tools we actually want to use ourselves.
