Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

RiTym Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the software applications developed and published by RiTym Technologies, including EXOS WAYFINDER™ (com.exos.wayfinder), EXOS GPS™ (com.ritym.exosgps), EXOS Station, and related desktop/embedded tools.

Offline-First Privacy Guarantee: All navigation algorithms, compass sensor fusion, satellite positioning, trail breadcrumb recording, and waypoint databases operate 100% locally on your physical device. We do not collect, track, store on remote cloud servers, or monetize your location coordinates or journey logs.

1. Location Data & Permissions

Our applications access location data (Precise GPS / GNSS and Coarse Network location) strictly to provide core navigation and positioning features:

  • Live Heading & Velocity: Calculating Course-Over-Ground (COG), Doppler velocity, altitude, and steering vectors to target waypoints.
  • Trail Backtrack & Safe Point: Recording local GPS breadcrumbs during active trek sessions so you can retrace your steps in wilderness areas.
  • Foreground Service Operation: When active trail recording is initiated, a persistent Android foreground notification informs you that GPS logging is running in device memory.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Location data is processed ephemerally in device RAM and stored exclusively in private local SQLite databases. It is never transmitted across external network servers.

2. Bluetooth & Nearby Device Communications

Our mobile applications utilize Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Bluetooth Classic interfaces solely for local device-to-device communication:

  • EXOS Hardware & GPS Bridge: Connecting to external GNSS transmitters, RTK receivers, and EXOS-Station microcontrollers to forward NMEA sentences into Android Mock Location providers.
  • Buddy Radar (Peer Tracking): Broadcasting and receiving localized, encrypted peer beacon packets over short-range BLE radio mesh without cellular network connectivity or internet access.

3. Sensor & Hardware Telemetry

The software accesses device magnetometers, accelerometers, gyroscopes, and barometric sensors locally to compute tilt-compensated magnetic heading, pitch, roll, and elevation profiles. Sensor telemetry never leaves your hardware.

4. In-App Purchases & Financial Billing

All in-app purchases and subscriptions (such as EXOS Pro Lifetime or Annual Pass) are processed directly and securely through Google Play Billing Services. RiTym Technologies does not process, receive, or store your payment card details, billing addresses, or banking information.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, ad trackers, or data brokers in our applications. We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Since navigation logs and waypoints are saved exclusively to your device's local storage, you retain complete ownership and control. You can clear your data at any time by resetting waypoints within the app settings or uninstalling the application. For further inquiries, visit our Account & Data Management Page.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications comply with applicable global privacy standards and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
