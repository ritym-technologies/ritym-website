Security & Trust

We protect your location privacy and telemetry streams through secure-by-default software engineering.

🔒

Strict Location Privacy

EXOS GPS does not upload or share location coordinates to third-party cloud servers without your explicit, opt-in consent. Forwarding channels stream exclusively inside your designated local network limits.

🛡️

Google Play Integrity

Our Android client integrates the Google Play Integrity API to confirm that the package running is official, un-tampered, and operates inside a certified, clean environment.

✍️

Cryptographic Signatures

All Windows installation files (.MSI packages) are digitally signed with a verified code-signing certificate, and official Android builds are certified via the Google Play signing manager.

🚀

OWASP Best Practices

Our web code, API design patterns, and billing routes strictly follow OWASP recommendations to defend against remote execution exploits, parameter tampering, and injection risks.

Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure

We value the security community's support. If you discover a vulnerability or potential threat in EXOS GPS or RitYm Technologies infrastructure, please report it to us confidentially:

  • Email your report to: security@ritym.com
  • Do not publish vulnerability details before an official patch is coordinated and deployed.
  • Provide step-by-step instructions or test scripts to replicate the issues.
