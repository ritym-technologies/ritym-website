📊 3. Supported NMEA 0183 Sentences & Protocol Breakdown

RiTym positioning engines output standard NMEA 0183 v4.10 sentences compatible with all commercial marine chartplotters, GIS surveying software, and the EXOS platform.

Sentence Identifier Standard Definition Output Frequency Key Data Elements
$GNGGA Global Positioning System Fix Data 10Hz (Configurable up to 25Hz) UTC time, latitude, longitude, fix status (3D/DGPS), satellite count, HDOP, altitude (MSL), geoid separation.
$GNRMC Recommended Minimum Navigation Information 10Hz UTC date & time, position status, speed over ground (knots), course over ground (degrees true), magnetic variation.
$GNVTG Course Over Ground and Ground Speed 10Hz Track true degrees, track magnetic degrees, ground speed in knots and kilometers per hour.
$GNGSA GNSS Dilution of Precision (DOP) & Active Sats 1Hz Operational mode (2D/3D), list of active satellite PRNs used for position solution, PDOP, HDOP, VDOP.
$GPGSV / $GLGSV / $GAGSV Satellites in View & Signal-to-Noise Ratio 1Hz Constellation breakdown (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou), satellite azimuth, elevation, and SNR (C/N0 in dB-Hz).
