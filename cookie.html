Cookie Policy

Learn how we use essential cookies on our web portal.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser to retain configuration choices and session preferences.

2. Essential Cookies

We use cookies exclusively to save your layout preferences (such as light or dark theme mode) and secure your sessions in the project configurator estimator wizard and customer portal.

3. Third-party Tracking

We do not implement behavioral tracking, third-party analytics pixels, or advertisement remarketing tags on this website.
