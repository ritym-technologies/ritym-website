💡 Essential Concepts for Beginners & Operators

🎹
Input Source vs Microphone:

MIDI Input is digital data instructions (like typing keys on a computer keyboard), NOT recorded audio sound from a mic.

📥
RX (Received Messages):

Increments when external devices (like the Virtual Keyboard) send notes into PulseStage. Pressing keys increases RX, NOT TX!

📤
TX (Transmitted Messages):

Increments when PulseStage sends commands (preset changes, parameter sliders, clock ticks) to pedals or synthesizers.

🔇
Silent Loopback is Valid:

MIDI carries control instructions, not audio waveform sound. A test can completely PASS even if your PC speakers are silent!
